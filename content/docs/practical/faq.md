### When do facilities open?

Estimates are usually provided by Barratt, the developer, during the North West
and West Cambridge Community Forums. _Last updated in June 2026._

Library
: Estimated opening time: late 2026 or early 2027

NHS Centre
: Unknown. Fit-out contract work expected to start in summer 2026.

Retail units on the Plaza
: Occupation planned for autumn 2026.

Sports Pavilion
: Transfer to City Council estimated for July/August 2026.

Allotments (Parcel BDW2)
: Transfer to City Council estimated for July/August 2026.

Livies Kinder Café
: Unknown. The project received planning permission in July 2025. Check out
[the Livies' website][livies] for the latest information.

Potential primary school in Darwin Green 1
: Unknown. Pending a decision from the Department of Education about what to do
with the site.

Secondary school in Darwin Green 2
: 2028 [or later][school_bbc]

[livies]: https://www.livies.co.uk/
[school_bbc]: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm2xl3n4pxdo

### When is the work expected to be completed?

Phase 1 (parcel BDW1), local centre
: Complete

Phase 2 (parcel BDW2)
: In progress, to be completed in 2026

Phase 3 (parcel BDW4)
: In progress, to be completed in 2027

Phase 4 (parcel BDW3)
: Due to start in summer 2026

The site should be complete by the end of 2031.
