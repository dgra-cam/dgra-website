## General rule

**Darwin Green is a no on-street parking zone.** You cannot park your car
on-street or, of course, on the pavement. Parking restrictions are regularly
enforced and people parking on forbidden areas may get a fine.

## Where to park

### Recommended

- Obviously, residents should park their car(s) on their drives, in their
  garage, or on their allocated parking space.

- Anyone can park on the designated visitor spaces. These should be for
  temporary parking for visitors only, and should not be used by residents to
  park their cars on a regular basis.

- The Sports Pavilion in Darwin Green has a parking area with 20 spots, some of
  them with charging stations. The parking is open to the public and free to
  use.

### Unsure

Some people have been using the parking space near Chapter House at the end of
Lawrence Weaver Road, although it's not clear whether this is permitted or not.

### Not recommended

Be careful at the junction of Galton Road and Patagonia Way: it seems that the
big pebble stones (also known as "dinosaur eggs") on the side of the road are
not visible enough, and multiple incidents involving cars over these stones
have occurred. It is Not Recommended to park here.
