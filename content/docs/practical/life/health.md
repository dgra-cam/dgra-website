## Doctors/GP services

Anyone in England can register with a GP surgery to access NHS services. It's
free to register. You do not need proof of address or immigration status, ID or
an NHS number. For further information, look at [the NHS page on how to
register with a GP surgery][nhs-gp]. Here are some practices in the area:

[nhs-gp]: https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/how-to-register-with-a-gp-surgery/

* [Huntingdon Road Surgery](https://www.huntingdonroadsurgery.nhs.uk/)
  (1 Huntingdon Road CB3 0DB)
* [Girton Branch Surgery](https://www.huntingdonroadsurgery.nhs.uk/),
  another branch of the Huntingdon Road Surgery
  (1a Peppys Way CB3 0PA)
* [Bridge Street Medical Centre](https://www.bridgestreetmedicalcentre.com/)
  (2 All Saints Passage CB2 3LS)
* [Red House Surgery](https://www.redhousesurgery.nhs.uk/)
  (96 Chesterton Road CB4 1ER)

You can also use NHS's 111 for non-emergency situations to discuss your symptoms,
and you can chose option 2 in a mental health situation.

{{% pageinfo color="warning" %}}
In case of life-threatening emergency, dial 999.
{{% /pageinfo %}}
