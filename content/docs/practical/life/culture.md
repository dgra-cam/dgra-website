Cambridge City Council plays a major role in supporting the development of art
and culture in the city. Details about how the Council works and supports local
artists can be found on the [Council's website][arts].

Cambridge hosts several nationally recognised venues including the Corn
Exchange and the Junction. More details can be found on the [Cambridge Live
website][camblive].

Cambridge Open Studios is an organisation that brings artists and the public
together to show and sell their art. This happens every year for all weekends
in July. For more details, see [Cambridge Open Studios' website][cos].
