## For older people

There are some local groups and activities in community and faith spaces close
by but you may also be interested in exploring the following:

* [Forever Active](https://www.foreveractive.livingsport.co.uk/) - Provides
  exercise and activity sessions for the over 50 age groups within
  Cambridgeshire
* [Care Network Cambridgeshire](https://care-network.org.uk) - Provides help
  for older, isolated and vulnerable people enabling them to remain independent
  and maintain social contact with friends and the community
* [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/cambridgeshireandpeterborough/) - Works
  with and for older people across Cambridgeshire and Peterborough, providing a
  wide range of services and support
* [University of the 3rd Age (U3A) Cambridge](https://www.u3ac.org.uk/) - A
  voluntary organisation that offers over 300 courses and activities for
  retired and semi-retired people in Cambridge

## For young people

* [Romsey Mill](https://www.romseymill.org/) - Youth clubs
* [Girton Colts](https://girtoncolts.co.uk/)
  (5-12 years) - Girls and boys football
* [Sea Cadets](https://www.sea-cadets.org/cambridge) (12-18 years)
* [Rainbows and Girlguides](https://www.girlguiding.org.uk/get-involved/finding-your-nearest-unit/)
  (4-18 years, girls)
* [Woodcraft Folk - Elfins](https://woodcraft.org.uk/groups/evergreen-elfins/)
  (6-9 years) at Storey's Field Centre
* [Beavers, Cubs and Scouts, Girton](https://www.girtonscouts.org.uk/)
  (6-14 years)
* [Beavers, Cubs and Scouts, Histon](https://www.histon-scouts.org.uk/)
  (6-14 years)
* [Storey's Field Centre](https://www.storeysfieldcentre.org.uk/) has several
  groups for children (various ages)
* [Centre 33](https://www.centre33.org.uk) - City based support for young
  people
* [Kooth](https://www.kooth.com) - Online support for young people
* [Pinpoint](https://www.pinpoint-cambs.org.uk/) - For children with SEND needs
