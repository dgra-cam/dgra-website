There are number of faith spaces both locally and across the city, many of
which also run community activities. These include:

* [New Life Church](https://www.newlifechurchcambridge.org/)
* [St Augustine's Church](https://stas.org.uk/)
* [Cambridge Chinese Christian Church](https://www.cccc.org.uk/)
* [Castle Street Methodist Church](https://castlestreet.org.uk/)
* [Girton Baptist Church](https://www.girtonbaptistchurch.org.uk/)
* [Cambridge Central Mosque](https://cambridgecentralmosque.org/)

The University of Cambridge holds a useful [Directory of Faith and Belief
Communities in Cambridge][faithdir].

The [City Council's Equality Groups pages][equality] also hold some useful
information about groups supporting different faiths, ethnicities, those who
identify as LGBTQIA+, and those living with disabilities.

[faithdir]: https://www.equality.admin.cam.ac.uk/projects/faith-and-belief-practice/directory-faith-and-belief-communities-cambridge
[equality]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/equality-groups
