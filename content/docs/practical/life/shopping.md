## In Darwin Green

Darwin Green is expected to host a couple of retail shops as well as a
supermarket, but as of this writing none of these are ready, yet.

## Grocery

There are a number of small and large stores nearby to get your essentials and
for a larger grocery shop.

* [Sainsbury's](https://stores.sainsburys.co.uk/2709/cambridge-eddington-avenue)
  (27 Eddington Avenue CB3 1SE)
* [Co-op](https://www.coop.co.uk/store-finder/CB3-0LW/120-girton-road)
  (120 Girton Road CB3 0LW)
* [Post Office - Hazelwood Close](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/branch-finder/2841134/hazelwood-close)
  (1A Hazelwood Close, Cambridge, CB4 3SN)
* [Post Office - West Cambride university](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/branch-finder/0111139/west-cambridge-university)
  (15JJ Thomson Avenue CB3 0US)

## Other local shops

Near the junction of Histon Road and Windsor Road (near the Esso garage) you
will find a variety of local businesses and handy places to shop including a
pharmacy, international grocers, coffee shop, Co-op, Aldi, Iceland, Each
charity shop and takeaways. Explore and support the local businesses where you
can!
