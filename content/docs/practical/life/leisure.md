## Community facilities

Darwin Green has Community Rooms located in the Plaza (also known as Evolution
Court) on Galton Road. Queries can be emailed to
<info.dgrooms@cambridge.gov.uk>. The rooms are available for hire, see the
[City Council page for the rooms][comrooms] for details.

Other Community facilities nearby include Storey's Field, William Collyn
Community Centre and the Meadows Community Centre. There is a list of
facilities across the city on the [Council's page for community
centres][comcenters].

[comrooms]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/darwin-green-community-rooms
[comcenters]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/community-centres

## Libraries

Along with what you might expect at your local library, they can also be a huge
source of local information, knowledge, events and activities! Your closest
library will be on Darwin Green itself, but has not opened yet. In the
meantime, Darwin Green hosts regular visits from [the Mobile Library][moblib].

Consult the [list of librairies on the County Council's website][list-librairies] for more information about other libraries that you may want to visit in the city and
beyond.

[moblib]: https://www.cambridgeshire.gov.uk/residents/libraries-leisure-culture/libraries/visit-a-library/mobile-libraries
[list-librairies]: https://www.cambridgeshire.gov.uk/residents/libraries-leisure-culture/libraries

## Sports and leisure

Darwin Green will include a tennis court and football pitch as well as a sports
pavilion.

There are also a wide range of sport and leisure facilities across the city, see [the City Council's webpage for sport and fitness facilities][sports].

[sports]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/sport-and-fitness-facilities

## Parks and open spaces

In addition to the open spaces and play areas provided at Darwin Green, there
are also other areas nearby including Eddington lakes and Jesus Green.
Playgrounds include Eddington Avenue playground, Histon Road Recreation Ground
and Girton Play area on Cambridge Road.

For a full map of parks and playgrounds in the city visit [the map on Cambridge City Council's website][parks].

[parks]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/map-of-parks-and-playgrounds
