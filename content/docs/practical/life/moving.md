## Getting around

### Maps and navigation

When it comes to updates, not all map services are equal. Google Maps, for
example, only gets updates every few years, meaning that delivery drivers,
friends, and family may struggle to find an address using a recently created
postcode. [OpenStreetMap][osm] is the most up-to-date mapping tool, with a good
portion of Darwin Green already mapped.

The [what3words] application may be useful for deliveries, too.

See also [our interactive map of the area][dgmap].

[osm]: https://www.openstreetmap.org/
[what3words]: https://www.what3words.com
[dgmap]: /docs/lore/map/

### Car share schemes

Enterprise Car Club is the official car club provider on behalf of Cambridge
City Council and Cambridgeshire County Council. For more information, visit
[the City Council's dedicated page][carclub].

[carclub]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/car-clubs

### Cycling

Cambridge is one of the best places in the country for cycling. For more
information on cycling in Cambridge, visit [the County Council's
website][county] and search "cycling".

[county]: https://www.cambridgeshire.gov.uk

## Public transport

### Bus

Plan your local bus route by visiting [Stagecoach's
map][stagecoach] or downloading the Stagecoach app.

Stagecoach runs most bus services in the city and you can catch the Citi 5 or 6
from Huntingdon Road or the Citi 8 from Histon Road (opposite Aldi) which takes
you into the centre of Cambridge. From the city you can then catch various
buses to other areas of the city and beyond.

You can catch the Whippet bus (turn right out of the estate at the junction
between Huntingdon Road and Lawrence Weaver Road, near the BP garage) from
Huntingdon Road to Addenbrookes on the U1 or U2. For more information, look at
[Whippet's website][whippet].

The closest park-and-ride is at Madingley Road, CB3 0EX and will take you into
the centre of Cambridge; or you can catch the bus A to the park-and-ride in
Addenbrookes from Histon Road (opposite Aldi). For more details, see [Cambridge
Park & Ride website][parkandride].

[stagecoach]: https://www.stagecoachbus.com/maps
[whippet]: https://www.whippetbus.co.uk/times/
[parkandride]: https://www.cambridgeparkandride.info

### Rail

Cambridge is well connected by rail. There are regular trains from Cambridge
Stations to London King's Cross, Ipswich, Ely, Norwich, Brighton, Birmingham
and Stansted. The stations are:

* **Cambridge Station**, near the city centre (Station Road CB1 2JW)
* **Cambridge North** (Cowley Road CB4 0WZ)
* Cambridge South is [under construction][train-south]

[train-south]: https://www.networkrail.co.uk/running-the-railway/our-routes/anglia/improving-the-railway-in-anglia/cambridge-south-station/
