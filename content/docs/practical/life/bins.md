### Bins and recycling

All household and communal bins in Cambridge are collected by Cambridge City
Council.

Check out the [collection days for the different bins][bins]

For individual household bins, please put your bins out at your nearest
collection point or at the front of your property before 6 am.

Moving into a new home often causes additional waste such as boxes, cardboard
etc. The normal bin collections will not take this waste away, so please
dispose of it by taking it to a local recycling point. You can also arrange for
bulky items to be collected. There is a charge for this service.

For all information regarding bins, recycling, bulky waste collections and
reducing your waste, take a look at the [page on bins and recycling on the
Council's website][recycling].
