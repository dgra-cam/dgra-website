## Garden allotments in Cambridge

There are several allotment sites in the city. Some are managed by Cambridge
City Council and some have their own associations. Details are available on [Cambridge City Council's website][allotments].

[allotments]: https://www.cambridge.gov.uk/allotment-sites

## Darwin Green garden allotments

As of this writing, the garden allotments in Darwin Green have not been
completed. Nonetheless, he waiting list for booking an allotment is open on the
City Council's website.
