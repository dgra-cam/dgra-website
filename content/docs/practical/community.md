The residents, the users, and the friends of Darwin Green, Franklin Gardens,
and King's Court and their facilities form the Darwin Green community.

The community is also active on **Facebook group <i class='fa-brands

### <i class='fa-brands fa-whatsapp'></i> WhatsApp

There is also a **community chat** on **<i class='fa-brands fa-whatsapp'></i>
WhatsApp**, available to residents and neighbours, on invitation. If you live
in or around Darwin Green and would like to request an invitation, please
**[contact us][contact]**. Please mention that you agree to follow the code of
conduct for the chat, and don't forget to specify the number you use for
WhatsApp. Here's the code of conduct:

> - Be polite and kind
> - Do not send spam
> - Use first names only
> - Do not swear; keep the chat family-friendly
> - Never (ever!) talk about politics
> - There is zero tolerance to aggression or anti-social behaviour in the chat or
>   in Darwin Green, Franklin Gardens, or King's Court; administrators may
>   enforce moderation by removing users from the chat
> - Stick to English, the common language of the chat

There are several hundred members in the chat, so treat it as public space when
you post information.

Here are some acronyms to understand what the community talks about:

: Barratt / David Wilson (now Barratt Redrow)

BR
: Barratt Redrow

BSG
: Buy/Sell/Gift chat group

DGMC
: Darwin Green Management Company, a RMC managed by residents and taking care
  of the [HANDLE][map-names] blocks as well as Barratt's sales office.

: Friendly Neighbours chat group

: Resident Management Company
