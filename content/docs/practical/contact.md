## Darwin Green Development

Report any issue with the development or construction works to Barratt Redrow.

Note that there is a Planning Officer drop-in at the Darwin Green Community
Rooms every last Wednesday of the month, from 10 am to 12 pm. You can ask your
questions to the Planning Officer who ensures that the development's
construction goes according to the approved plans.

If you're interested in buying or renting, contact Barratt Redrow at the Sales
and Marketing Suite or L&Q (for renting).

## Anti-social behaviour

If you witness any anti-social behaviour, please report it to the authorities.
The police can take no action to improve the safety of the neigbourhood if they
get no reports.

Report anti-social behaviour to the police via the [Cambridgeshire Constabulary
website][police-report]. You can also report anti-social behaviour, as well as
a number of other issues, on [Cambridge City Council's website][camcc-report].

For information on how to make your neigbourhood safer contact
<cambnhw@gmail.com>, the Neigbourhood Watch Coordinator
