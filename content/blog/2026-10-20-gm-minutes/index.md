## Active Lifestyle Officer

Maysie introduced herself to the residents, as the new Active Lifestyle Officer
for the City Council, covering North-West Cambridge. This is an opportunity for
people to find ways to exercise. Maysie tries to understand what the community
wants. Some activities are funded by Barratt, other through grants; not all
activities are free but they're usually rather affordable. Once the Pavilion
has been handed over to the City, Maysie will be looking into organising
sessions such as meditation classes, etc. In the meantime, there will be some
free tennis coaching sessions during half-term. There should be "active family"
events in Darwin Green next summer.

## Planning Meetings

Simon Smith, new Chair of the [Planning Meetings], explained what these
meetings are about:
- They were initiated at the time of the foundation issues to bring Barratt and
  residents into one room, track issues and follow-up actions. Barratt was
  surprised by the degree of involvement from residents at first, and has been
  very cooperative since then.
- Now attendees include Barratt project managers, sometimes John from GCSP,
  residents (and residents associations) for Darwin Green and Franklin Gardens
  (DGRA), but also Windsor Road (WIRE) or Woodlark Road, Councillors.
- Example topics: ditch, layout of houses in Franklin Gardens, cycle ways,
  environmental issues, signage, lighting, ...
- Out of scope: higher-level planning such as schools (addressed at Community
  Forums).

Everyone is welcome to contribute, by joining the meetings in person, or by
contacting the association via WhatsApp or [the website](/contact).

## Updates

### Finance

- There is ~£2,300 on the bank account.
- Graham is trying to sort out public liability insurance; he's trying to check
  with Barratt (as owner of the land) about how responsibilities are shared.
- Most of the money comes from pitch fees from food trucks. One concern is
  whether the association is supposed to pay corporate tax on the fees it
  collects. Graham is investigating. It's unclear at the moment, and may depend
  on whether the money is spent on charitable endeavours.

### Grants

Konstantina is looking into the process for grant applications. The Council
offers several kinds of grants (up to £5k, £10k, £40k). The association needs
to have some specific policies in place to apply.

The next steps will be to figure out what projects to apply for, and then to
apply. If you know people interested or active in that area please spread the
word.

### Events

Faith is looking into organising a get-together event around Christmas.

The association is looking for volunteers to help, and to submit ideas to help
define what this event will look like.

Faith will also ask Barratt if it's possible to get a Christmas tree on the
Plaza (but hopefully one that looks better than last year's).

### Food trucks

Kiwi Coffee has been a success so far and proved very beneficial to the
community, and ensures a regular presence on the Plaza.

There was a debate on whether to raise pitch fees for food trucks. Currently,
food trucks pay £30 per venue to the association (Note: Kiwi Coffee comes more
often and pays a different price). Joanna tried to see how other communities
do, but our arrangement (the residents' association directly collecting the
fees) seems rather unique. In Trumpington, trucks pay from £40 to £60, but
there are three times as many residents.

In the end, Joanna will not raise the fees for now. First, the association will
see whether it needs to pay taxes on this, as this may impact the amount it
desires to collect. Another route for raising more money is to have more
frequent venues: starting next year, Joanna will experiment with food trucks on
one Saturday per month (in addition to the current schedule). She wants to try
new types of food, too.

Joanna may run a poll in WhatsApp to figure out what residents are interested
in (type, frequency).

Another question is whether to open the community rooms to allow people to sit
and eat (providing access to toilets, and shelter in winter). This would
require finding funds to book the Community Rooms; the association needs to see
if it can find a grant for this.

### WhatsApp

#### Atmosphere

Previous WhatsApp admins did a great job at moderation. Since the groups were
handed over to the association in May this year, admins have been looking for
the right balance between freedom of speech and moderation and this has
occasionally led to an unfriendly atmosphere in the chat. Admins are aware and
are now trying to take a more proactive stance for moderation. They also hope
that discussions following antisocial behaviour reports will soon have a
dedicated channel, rather than the _Friendly Neighbours_ group.

#### Buy Sell Gift chat updates

During the meeting, residents voted on whether the _Buy Sell Gift_ chat should:

1. Change to be buy, sell, gift, for local residents only (DG/FG/KC)
2. Change to be gift only, for local residents only (DG/FG/KC)
3. Remain unchanged (buy, sell, gift for people in the broader area)

The second proposition won the vote: the group will change soon to become
**gift-only, for local residents only**.

#### Policies

Konstantina announced that policies to help with WhatsApp moderation are in the
process, coming on the website soon.

## Neighbourhood Watch

Konstantina has been working with Carol to form a team to help with and
follow-up antisocial behaviour cases resolution. The team includes Alex, Andy,
Kristian (local residents), although at the moment it is just the initial stage
of the process. Hopefully by next meeting Konstantina will be able to ask
representatives to come and present what they do.

Alex emphasised the importance of reporting all ASB occurrences to the police:
"don't get discouraged, keep reporting, even if there's no apparent or
immediate resolution, in the end it matters a lot".

## Community Forum

The Community Forum was scheduled for 15 October, but has been postponed and
the new date has not been announced yet.

The association expects the usual set of updates on the library, the retail
units, the allotments, the schools, etc.

Retail units in particular are more and more pressing, and the communication
from Barratt on the topic is not transparent (the opening has been delayed
multiple times, who will own the shops is not clear (retail owners, or real
estate investor), it would be good to get more details. Residents asked whether
there was any planning obligation regarding a date for opening the units, but
Simon replied there were none.

The association also wants to ask an update on coming work to improve the
Plaza.

If you have other questions or concerns that you would like to express during
the Forum, but cannot attend in person (or online), get in touch.

## Any other business

Some other notes related to the developments:

- The consultation on the Howes Green planning applications closed on 13
  October and is now awaiting for the Council's Planning Committee's decision.

- The PR firm working with Barratt reached out to contribute towards a book
  club (hosting events in show rooms, or donating books). The association
  doesn't know any book club to point them to at the moment, but if anyone
  wants to start one, get in touch.

- There has been a demand for post boxes in Darwin Green; but this was also
  raised at the Planning Meetings, and Barratt is already working on that case.

- Residents have been asking about buses in Darwin Green. Alice and Rory
  reported that two new lines, U3 and U4, between Addenbrooke and North
  Cambridge, are planned, and should have stops in Darwin Green (once the road
  between Darwin Green and Franklin Gardens allows it). Rory will share the
  related document if he can.
