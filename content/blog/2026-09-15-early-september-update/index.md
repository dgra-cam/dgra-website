It's September already, summer is ending, children are back at school, and...
lots has been happening in Darwin Green! Here are a few things that took place
over the last weeks.

## Street Play in King's Court

_Contributed by Faith_

On Sunday 6 September, families and neighbours came together at King's Court
for a relaxed afternoon of Street Play.

{{% imgproc street-play-chalk Resize "x800" %}}
Street Play in King's Court
{{% /imgproc %}}

Children and adults enjoyed giant chess, Connect 4, Jenga and other games and
play activities. One of the nicest things about the afternoon was how calm and
easy-going it felt. It was lovely to see the green space filled with children
playing, families spending time outdoors together, and neighbours stopping for
a chat. We saw a lot of happy faces!

Street Play was organised as an opportunity for the wider Darwin Green
community to enjoy a shared space differently and create more opportunities for
neighbours to meet. It was encouraging to see people of different ages joining
in, whether they came specifically for the event or stopped by to see what was
happening.

{{% imgproc street-play-organisers Resize "x600" %}}
Happy faces
{{% /imgproc %}}

A big thank you to Cambridge City Council for supporting the event and helping
to make it possible, and to L&Q for generously providing the games and play
equipment enjoyed by families throughout the afternoon.

Thank you as well to everyone who came along, brought their families, helped
out or simply stopped to say hello. Community moments can be as simple as that:
a few games, an open green space and people willing to come together are
enough.

We hope there will be more opportunities like this to play, connect and get to
know our neighbours across Darwin Green, King's Court, or Franklin Gardens.

## Coffee van

In other exciting news, we now have a **coffee van** on the Plaza in the
morning! Shaun comes with his **Kiwi Coffee** van, **Wednesdays to Sundays**,
from 8 am to 2 pm. He sells coffee as well as a selection of cakes. The coffee
is great, and the cakes delicious. Come and give it a try!
