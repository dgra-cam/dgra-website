Here are the minutes for the General Meeting for DGRA from 14 July 2026. Many
thanks to all who attended!

## Welcome and introduction

Attendees:

- **DGRA Committee**: Konstantina Stamati (Chair), Graham Steed (Treasurer),
  Quentin Monnet (Secretary), Mel Hersey
- **Official representatives**: Rory Clark (County Councillor), Ann Muston
  (Girton Parish Councillor)
- 7 residents, from Darwin Green and Franklin Gardens; 1 dog

Faith Oyepeju and Joanna Gould (both from the Committee) sent apologies.

## Chair's update

### Stakeholders engagement

The association has been working on establishing contacts with different
stakeholders. This includes:

- Communicating with **Barratt**, following the Community Forum and drop-in
  sessions from the GCSP Planning Officer
- Approaching and meeting **Alex Sage**, new Cambridge City Councillor for
  Castle Ward
- Approaching and meeting with representatives from the **Eddington Residents
  Association**
- Meeting people from Rural Solutions, the **landscape consultants** from
  Barratt for the audit regarding the trees and plants
  - Vegetation has overgrown on the allotments (BDW2), they'll need some care
    and cleaning before the handover completes
  - The electricity hub on the allotments is not protected enough, lots of
    children may play in the area, and it can be a safety hazard.
  - The consultants were still to transmit their finalised report to Barratt.

### Community initiatives

The association promised £750 for a **Junior ParkRun in Darwin Green**, but
we're waiting for the organiser to come back to us. Last we heard, they were
still missing some funds.

### Development updates

See [updates from the latest Community Forum][comfor]: handover for the Sports
Pavilion, Allotments, and Library are all supposed to happen during summer,
although the Central Park's handover has been delayed because Anglian Water
needs some easement (and the Council wants nothing to do with it).

[comfor]: {{< ref "/blog/2026-06-12-community-forum-report/" >}}

Questions/Answers:

- Can we have a **sign post on Huntingdon Road** to signal the cut through? ->
  Maybe doable, we should ask.

- There's a safety issue for people crossing the road near that cut through --
  it needs a **pelican crossing**. This takes a long time to get sorted so we'd
  need to start thinking about it now.

- Also people would like **another crossing between Darwin Green and
  Eddington**, where it's leading to the physics labs. It might be done by the
  University, they have some ownership on the road.

- Will there be **a hill in the park** like on commercial pictures? -> Unlikely
  to happen from Barratt, the landscape is done and they don't want to touch
  anything (except for tree maintenance) until they hand over to the Council.

- Residents found **rubbish or tools** (including sharp/cutting tools such as
  saws or razor blades!) or a mini skip full of materials removed from houses
  left by Barratt workers outside of the fenced site, and reported it to
  Barratt. Let us (or Barratt) know when you see something wrong. Similarly,
  for any **noise complaints** related to the work, for example outside of
  work hours (from 8 am to 6 pm Monday--Friday, from 8 am to 1 pm on
  Saturday), take a video with date and time and report to Barratt or the
  GCSP Planning Officer.

- Vandalised bins have been reported; the bins around library corner are to be
  moved.

- Transportation: **What about buses**?
    - We need to push. Many people work at the University but live on Darwin
      Green's side of Huntingdon and are not willing to drive, it could work.
    - We could maybe have U1, 5, or 6 stop in Darwin Green.
    - Ann mentioned the association should also talk with Girton about buses.
      There was a big meeting about buses in Girton, but nobody's really
      ready to take it on, perhaps we should all join forces.

- Ann raised multiple times that communication was essential between the
  stakeholders in multiple areas. Girton is very close to Cambridge and the
  lack of communication may lead to duplicated efforts, wasted money, or
  insufficient resources for lack of working together on some issues.

Barratt has communicated that they have a **referral scheme** in place, by
which home owners in Darwin Green and Franklin Gardens can **claim £1,000** if
they introduce people to Barratt, and this leads to a home purchase. (Note that
this sum is available only once per household, and the scheme is not available
to tenants). Barratt explained that the idea is to encourage people to bring in
their friends and family to grow a strong community. Requests for details, and
claims, should be addressed to Barratt's sales office. The association passes
on the information, but is not otherwise involved in this referral scheme.

### Association infrastructure

Don't hesitate to **reach out** at <hello@dgra.cam>, or via the website's
contact form, so the association can get an idea about residents' concerns and
priorities. The association wants to take your concerns to Barratt or
authorities on your behalf to get more weight, through one official channel
rather than dispersing the message over several avenues.

The association is also looking into setting up an organisational framework to
ensure that future Committees can take over easily, when the time comes. Along
with that, it also needs a set of policies that will help define its process,
and should help with grant applications in the future.

### Anti-social behaviour

Konstantina has started exploring a few things to help organise the community
to tackle anti-social behaviour (ASB):

- We talked with ERA (Eddington Residents' Association); they work differently:
  they have a management community that can help, they have cameras at entry
  points of the site, and they can help recognise people committing ASB. They
  have connections with a police representative in the area (**_Coffee with a
  cop_**). At the moment, this liaison officer has retired and we're waiting
  for the next one; DGRA will try to liaise as well. It would be good to have
  _Coffee with a cop_ at the Community Rooms as well.

- Regarding the **Neighbourhood Watch**, Konstantina found two volunteers to
  help with the local scheme, and will chat with Carol, local coordinator for
  the Neighbourhood Watch. At this time it's tricky to get everybody to reply
  because of holidays, but activity will likely resume in September.

- The Council, the Neighbourhood Watch representative, and maybe the police
  are trying to organise a **dedicated community meeting** as soon as possible.

Questions/Answers and comments from attendees:

- Ann encouraged everybody to **report everything that happens**. Graham added
  that holidays are coming and ASB from kids will probably spike, and
  encouraged everyone to keep reporting, even when it seems to not lead to any
  reaction from the police.
- In defense of reporting, one resident explained that they reported a separate
  case and the person behind the ASB was arrested shortly after, so **reporting
  does pay off**.
- Rory noted that the police haven't run _Coffee with a cop_ recently because
  they're on reduced capacity at the moment. One resident attended a local
  session (outside of Darwin Green) six months ago: it was mostly about
  Franklin Garden's residents explaining their own issues.
- One resident complained about recurrent ASB in their building, in spite of
  numerous reports to the police, Councillors, or the relevant L&Q person. Rory
  explained that the person at L&Q hasn't responded to Councillors either
  (Councillors will try their superiors), and offered to take it away.

## Treasurer's update: bank and finances

Graham reported that due to the bank's requirements, the **transfer of the bank
account** has been slow, but this is now done. He followed up with invoices for
the food truck owners for the last months (May, June). Accounts are ready.

There is **about £1,820 on the account**, coming mostly from the pitch fee from
food truck owners. Some of this is committed to the Junior ParkRun, but this
still leaves over £1k so the association can help fund things. Talks are
beginning about some potential social events.

One of the next steps will be to look into **insurance**, necessary for food
trucks and for other events the association might contribute to. There will
also be some **policies/process** to determine who in the Committee can spend,
up to what amount, and so on.

## Secretary's update: contacts, website, WhatsApp

Quentin has initiated **communications with various associations**, on behalf
of DGRA: ERA (Eddington), WIRE (Windsor Road), ORRA (Oxford Road), FeCRA
(residents' associations federation), FOHRR (Friends of Histon Road Recreation
Ground). Some of these were just to say hi, others already led to in-person
discussions about possible synergies.

Quentin has updated **the website** with the Constitution adopted during the
AGM, and reworked some sections, in particular to display more information
about the association. He also maintains the list of people to notify by email
about future meetings.

The handover of **the WhatsApp groups** to the association has been completed,
the same rules remain in place. The main group got about 60 new members due to
the AGM. This is due in part to Mel who advertised the community group on
social media.

This is also due to Quentin and Konstantina reaching out to Barratt to ship
**welcome leaflets** in the "welcome packs" containing documents that they
leave in new residents' homes. Barratt kindly agreed to print and ship the
leaflets, and new residents confirmed they received it. The association is
thankful to Barratt for shipping the leaflets, and to Tom, who helped with the
graphic design.

## Social media

The handover for the **Facebook group** administration (PLAZA) is complete, the
group was mostly left as it was. We're still learning; there has been some spam
in the group, and Mel is trying to keep an eye on it.

New pages were set up **on Facebook, Instagram, X, Threads**. Mel has been
sharing events on these.

There has been a discussion about **what to do with the Buy/Sell/Gift chat**.
Residents have complained about it being open to everybody, and some users
abusing it (too much posting resulting in other posts lost in the flood,
pick-up location too far away, buyers not showing up, and so on). It seems
worth redefining the scope for this group, even though some attendees raised
that Darwin Green neighbours also use and benefit from the chat; others raised
that there are other marketplaces (such as on Facebook) available in the
broader area anyway. The association will run a WhatsApp poll to determine what
people prefer. The precise options, and the fate of the existing group (if we
were to create a new one), are still to be defined.

## Food trucks

Handover was already complete for the AGM; it just keeps going. It is now
**time to review the list of food trucks** in the schedule; Joanna is open to
suggestions (reach out if you have some). New trucks need a 5-star hygiene
rating. The review will also cover the amount of the pitch fee (currently £30).

One attendee asked for **a coffee truck in the morning**.

We tried once in the past to **open the Community Rooms** when a food truck was
here so that people could sit and chat. Only 14 people came, but it was not
well advertised at the time. The Council said they can still pay (for the
rooms) for one other occurrence: the association needs to follow up and find a
date to give it another try.

## Events organisation

Faith has been running several **events for children** (see association website
and calendar).

The location for the outdoors **table tennis** table is still in discussion
with Barratt.

For this summer, the association is looking at some **late summer social
event** (in September?). It first needs running accounts (now done) and
insurance (in progress). Various ideas have been floated: it could be some sort
of international day where people bring dishes from their home country to
share, and children might dress up. More information to follow.

There has been a discussion between Faith and the Council about the association
"enhancing" events in Darwin Green from the Council, and having **a notice
board** to display everything happening in the area. The association also plans
to add a dedicated page on its website to promote events to come. These are
ideas at this stage, nothing concrete yet.

Ann raised that she wants to open some buildings to the community in Girton so
that people can use them, if it rains, or if they need space, for example, and
this could happen in Darwin Green when the library opens, maybe; but it needs
forward planning.

## Call for volunteers

There are a lot of things that need to come in place, for example for doing
events, so **volunteers are most welcome**. One area that needs people is
events organisation. Another is the "Friends of Central Park" group (attached
to the association), for defining and run actions to improve the park or the
landscape in the development. Reach out if you're interested!

## Call for feedback

The current Committee is still defining a vision for the area, and the
association, and will hopefully be able to present more of this at the next
meeting. But it really needs residents' input, so please do **reach out to
share your feedback, concerns, ideas, or priorities**!

## Future meetings

There was a discussion on the time of the meeting, 8 pm being possibly a better
option for people coming back from work, although we don't want to end too
late, either. Another proposal was to make them coincide with the food truck
days. No decision yet.

Note there will be a public **consultation on the Darwin Green Academy**
(future secondary school in Darwin Green 2&3) on Thursday 16 July from 4:30 pm
to 7:30 pm at the Darwin Green Community Rooms.

**The next General Meeting is scheduled for 12 October** (time to be
confirmed).

Thanks again to all who helped organise and attended the meeting!
