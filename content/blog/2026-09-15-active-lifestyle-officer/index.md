_Maysie says:_

“Hello everyone,

My name is Maysie Stedman, and I'm the new Active Lifestyle Officer at
Cambridge City Council with a dedication to the North-West area.

My role is all about helping people lead healthier, more active lives and
making it easier for communities to access opportunities that support their
wellbeing. Whether that's connecting people with local activities, working
alongside community groups and partners, or helping create initiatives that
encourage people to get moving, I'm passionate about making activity accessible
and enjoyable for everyone.

One of the things I enjoy most about my role is meeting people from different
communities and understanding what matters to them. No two days are the same,
and I love being able to support projects and ideas that can have a positive
impact on people's health, confidence, and overall quality of life.

I'm looking forward to working with residents, partners, and local communities
across the North-West. If you'd like to find out more about what I do or
explore ways we can work together, I'd be delighted to hear from you.”
