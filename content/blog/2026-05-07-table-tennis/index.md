[Elenasy CIC][elenasy], which already runs [LEGO and board games][clubs]
sessions for young people here on Darwin Green, has been awarded a [Sport
England] grant to bring **table tennis** to the community.

The grant covers **a permanent outdoor table** and **two portable tables for
indoor sessions**. Conversations are ongoing between the Council and Barratt
about where the outdoor table will be sited, so watch this space for more
information.

The two portable indoor tables will be available in the meantime: one aimed at
getting adults moving, and another at giving teenagers something to do that
isn't a screen.

The initiative came out of conversations between residents, with interest
picked up through [the Friendly Neighbours WhatsApp group][whatsapp], so it's
fair to say this one came from the community itself. Weekly sessions are
planned to start in May. Get ready!

If you would like to know more or get involved, you can reach Faith at Elenasy
on <hello@elenasy.org>.
