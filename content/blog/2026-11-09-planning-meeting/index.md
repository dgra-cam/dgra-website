## Action point updates

### Highway and street lightning

**Lamp posts and street lighting**: ISSUE long standing complaints about
outages and lights shining directly into bedrooms. Contractor is failing to
undertake rolling audits and repair / replace outages. :green_circle: ACTION:
Alice to make a formal complaint and send mapped details to customer care email
southmidlands@newhomecare.co.uk for correct response and allocation of
resources.

**Greenway temporary pedestrian / cycle route**: White line cycle symbols
removed, and multiple cyclists dismount signs erected. This instruction is not
being enforced. Matt has confirmed the full pedestrian / cycle route will be
completed by September 2027. It will comprise new footpaths on both sides of
the existing route which will become a dedicated cycle way.

**Franklin Gardens parking enforcement**: At last meeting Andrew confirmed the
parking enforcement process---notification of residents, installation of
signage and one week grace period---will be progressed shortly. :green_circle:
ACTION Simon to ask Tom to provide update.

**Franklin Garden Street name signage**: :green_circle: UPDATED ACTIONS 1)
Simon to ask Tom to provide an update and send Quentin the approved plan. 2)
Quentin to ask residents for any remaining issues via Friendly Neighbours.

**Franklin Gardens pedestrian and cycle links**: 1) Brownlow Road, yellow lines
to protect route from parked vehicles, 2) Blackhall Road, kerb to be removed
for smooth transition. :green_circle: ACTION Simon to ask Tom to provide
update.

**BDW2 unmade up roads**: Matt has reported, BDW currently mobilising top
surfacing works with imminent commencement. :green_circle: ACTION Simon to ask
Matt to confirm timetable for notification of dates and requests to remove
vehicles from the highway to the allow the works. Parking restrictions will be
suspended to allow residents to park outside BDW2.

**BDW2 highway hazards**: :green_circle: ACTION Alice and other residents to
report broken kerbs or other hazards to [customer care email] for correct
response and allocation of resources.

**BDW2 Jenyns Drive**: (Alice to confirm other relevant street) Highway ponding
due to blocked gutter drains. :green_circle: ACTION Alice to report precise
locations with photos to [customer care email] (copy to Matt Johnson).

**BDW2 to Huntingdon Road, pedestrian / cycle access and light controlled
crossing**:
1. Access route: to be subject of DDA compliance works and signage,

## Any other business

### Darwin Green Phase 1

**BDW website for Darwin Green** (`www.ourdarwingreen.co.uk`) remains pending.
:green_circle: ACTION: Simon to ask Will Hodgson for progress update.

**Library opening** planned for Q1 27, Rory to confirm opening days and times.

**Primary and secondary schools**: Rory reported the secondary and primary
schools at Darwin Green 2 are delayed as BDW's proposed provision of water,
drainage and power from Darwin Green 1 would be insufficient for requirements.
The County Council will hold the Darwin Green 1 primary school site pending
evidence of demand arising from new development.

**Shops**: John reported applications to be submitted for external ducting.

**Neighbourhood Watch**: in discussion, details of Neighbourhood Watch and
local contacts to be posted on the DGRA website. Sue J. recommended the
[Fearless] website to parents of teenage children.

**Visitor parking**: Recurring concern that residents are using visitor parking
spaces. :green_circle: ACTION DGRA to consult on visitor parking management
options. :green_circle: ACTION Residents can report incidents to Barratt's
[customer care email].

**Public art strategy**: John reported BDW will be consulting residents on a
strategy and action plan.

**Allotments**: Waiting list of 73 Darwin Green residents, 75 squared-meter
allotments to be laid out with 0.5m pathways. Phillipa celebrated the health
and well-being benefits of allotments. :green_circle: ACTION Simon to establish
whether applicants are allocated allotments closest to their homes.

**Bins review**: In discussion there are too many bins in some places and too
few in others, notably dog waste bins. :green_circle: ACTION: DGRA to undertake
review. :green_circle: ACTION Simon to inform Tom about misuse of box rear of
the Franklin Gardens sales office.
