## You are invited

The Darwin Green Residents' Association (DGRA) will hold its **Annual General
Meeting** on **Tuesday 12 May 2026, at 7 pm**, in the Darwin Green Community
Rooms.

All residents and neighbours are warmly invited to attend!

## DGRA area

The association aims to represent residents of **Darwin Green**, **King's
Court**, and **Franklin Gardens**, but the meeting is open to everyone.

## Agenda

- Welcome & introductions
- Background and purpose of DGRA
- Adoption of the new Constitution
- Election of Officers and Committee
- Bank & finance
- Key issues and priorities
- Any other business
- Next meetings

## Why come along?

Let's start with a bit of context. In Darwin Green, King's Court, and Franklin
Gardens, we're lucky to have a strong community, with many individuals
contributing their time and effort to make it a great place to live. Sometimes,
it is good to have a more formal structure to back community efforts: a
residents' association can help when it comes to applying for grants, or
carrying more weight in discussions with parties such as developers or local
authorities. DGRA has existed for a while, but the existing Committee is
standing down. Today, we're putting together a new team to define the direction
of the association for the years to come.

The AGM is the first step in this effort. For you, it can be a great
opportunity to get to know the new team behind DGRA. As a resident, you will
take part in the adoption of the new Constitution for the association. You will
also vote to elect the Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, and the other members of
the Committee. You're also welcome to nominate yourself, if you want to join
the adventure! After the new association is formed, you'll be able to weigh in
on the priorities for the association, and to shape its direction.

On the Committee side, we currently have candidates for the Chair, Secretary,
and Treasurer positions, with Konstantina S., Quentin M., and Graham S.
respectively volunteering for these roles. We are looking for a Communications
Officer (to handle social media) as well as regular Committee members who would
like to get involved in the association. In particular, we'd love to have
representatives from King's Court and Franklin Gardens. If you consider
nominating yourself for any position, please [get in touch](/contact).

**You can find the proposed Constitution for the association here:
[DGRA Constitution (provisional)](<DGRA Constitution (provisional).pdf>).**
Note that formal membership and voting will be reserved for the residents of
the area covered by the association. Of course, residents from neighbouring
areas are more than welcome to join and engage with the community.

In short: meet your neighbours and the DGRA team, and get involved to build a
strong association that supports and represents residents. Bring a neighbour
along!
