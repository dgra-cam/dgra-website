New website launch for the Darwin Green Residents’ Association (DGRA), and the Darwin Green community
{{% /imgproc %}}

Today, we officially launch our new website! It's meant to become a platform
for relaying events in Darwin Green, and for building a knowledge base that can
benefit to the residents. We hope you'll find it useful.

Here are a few highlights:

- Take a look at the calendar at the bottom of the [home page](/), to see the
  oncoming events in Darwin Green. Are you ready for the Plant Exchange at the
  end of this month?

- Keep informed about what's going on in the development, by reading our
  [blog posts](/blog/). Did you read
  [the minutes from the last Community
  Forum](/blog/2025-03-06-community-forum-report/)?

- Browse our [knowledge base](/docs/) to learn more about the area.
  For example, did you know
  [where the names of the streets come from](/docs/lore/names/)?

We hope to add more contents about the residents' association in the future
(for now... we've got a new [logo](/docs/practical/dgra/#logo)). In the
meantime, don't hesitate to [send feedback](/contact/). And all the code for
the website is [public on GitHub](https://github.com/dgra-cam/dgra-website). If
you have suggestions, fixes, or content to offer, please create an Issue or a
Pull Request on GitHub, or simply get in touch.

Enjoy the website, and see you soon in Darwin Green!
