During 2025, the developer and community in Darwin Green attained a number of
milestones. To name a few, we got electricity on the Plaza and the return of
many food trucks on Thursday nights, a diversification of the community events,
the DGRA website! And of course, the opening of the Central Park area.

Now it's time to turn the page, and to look ahead at what's coming in 2026.
Just for the first two months, there's a lot on the agenda! A number of events
focus on young people, but everyone should find something to participate in.
Hold on tight, and let's have a look.

## Special events: focus on young people

All taking place at the Darwin Green Community Rooms.

### Community Focus Group meeting: focus on youth

**Wednesday 28th January 2026, 6 pm to 7 pm**

The next Community Focus Group meeting will specifically focus on addressing
youth-related topics and concerns. This is an opportunity to have your voice
heard on matters affecting young people in our community, to help with
community events, or to propose new ones.

### Online safety session

**Monday 10th February 2026**

There will be a session about online safety for children. More details will be
shared soon, but if you're interested, you can already mark the date.

### Holiday lunch

**Wednesday 18th February 2026**

Children are invited for a free holiday lunch. This event is organised by
Cambridge City Council and [Cambridge Sustainable Food]. Check for more details
closer to the date, or take a look at [previous related posts][holiday-lunch].

[holiday-lunch]: /tags/for-children/
[Cambridge Sustainable Food]: https://cambridgesustainablefood.org/

### Youth survey

The Community team from Cambridge City Council plans to run a survey among
young people in Darwin Green and Franklin Gardens. Details should be
communicated from the Community Officer soon over the WhatsApp group, with the
intent of finding families with teenagers willing to take part in the survey.

## Other special events

### Coffee with a Cop

**Wednesday 14th January 2026, 10:30 am to 12:30 pm**

This is a great opportunity to meet your local police officers in a relaxed,
informal setting. You can ask questions, raise any local concerns, and get to
know the officers who serve our community. Drop in any time during the session.
[Find more information here][coffeecop].

[coffeecop]: https://www.cambs.police.uk/area/your-area/cambridgeshire/cambridge-city/cambridge-city-centre/meetings-and-events/our-priorities

### Photography exhibition or competition

Residents have shown interest in organising an event around photography, but
the details are not sorted out yet, so stay tuned.

## Development updates

Everyone hopes to see much more progress on the Darwin Green and Franklin
Gardens developments in 2026! Some of the expectations include:

- Opening of the retail shops on Darwin Green Plaza. Barratt said this would be
  for spring this year (although it had been previously announced for March
  2025). Will the prediction hold true, this time?
- Updates on the Sports Pavilion, the garden allotments, or the library
- An online platform for reporting problems. Rumour has it that Barratt planned
  to deliver the new website in January or soon after. It would be a good way
  to report (yet again) about the broken lights!

Note that the dates for the **North West and West Cambridge Community Forums**
[have been published][nwwccf]. The next forum will take place on **26th
February** in the evening, although the time and location have not been
communicated yet.

Another note on the Sports Pavilion: some sports organisations have already
been asking about hiring the pavilion for their activities. If this is what
you're after, the contact address to express an interest is
<ParksandRecreation@cambridge.gov.uk>.

## Weekly and recurring events

The Darwin Green Community Rooms host several regular activities throughout the
week (see also [our dedicated page][recurring]). The Saturday clubs are new
this year, while other events have been running for a while, but it's a good
time to get a refresher anyway! Remember that you can also find these events in
the Darwin Green calendar, on the homepage of this website.

[recurring]: /docs/practical/events/

### Mobile Library

**Monday 19th January 2026, 3 pm - 4:30 pm** and every third Monday of the
month

The mobile library will be visiting Darwin Green (on the Plaza). This is a
convenient way to borrow books, return items, and discover new reading material
without travelling to a library branch, while waiting for our own library to
open.

### Drop-in workspace

**Monday, 8:30 am - 5:30 pm**

Need a place to work? The Community Rooms offer free workspace on Mondays. For
more information, see [our dedicated page](/docs/practical/workspace/).

### Rhyme Time

_**Edit** As of end of February 2026, the Rhyme Time sessions have stopped._

### Community coffee

**Wednesday, 10:30 am - 12:30 pm**

Join your neighbours for community coffee. A relaxed social gathering where you
can meet people from the area and enjoy a cup of tea or coffee together.

### Planning Officer drop-in session

**Wednesday, 10:30 am - 12:30 pm**

Usually the first Wednesday of the month (check on the calendar if in doubt),
the local Planning Officer, and sometimes representatives of Barratt Redrow or
local Councillors, come to the Community Rooms for a discussion session with
residents of Darwin Green and its neighbourhood. This is a time to raise and
discuss concerns about the work in progress in the area. The Planning Officer
may or may not have answers or leverage to solve concerns from the residents,
but he can usually pass the word and help find the right person to contact.

### Food trucks

**Thursday, 5 pm - 8 pm**

Food trucks come to the Plaza every Thursday evening, offering a variety of
dining options. A great way to support local businesses and enjoy dinner
without cooking.

### Lego club and board games

Both activities are organised by [Elenasy] and supported by Cambridge City
Council. They target children aged 6 to 14, approximately. Parents are required
to stay with their children during these sessions. Please try booking in
advance by contacting <hello@elenasy.org>.

#### Lego club

**Saturday, 2 pm - 3 pm**

A play-based Lego club supporting STEM and engineering skills, confidence, and
problem-solving for young people.

#### Board games and learning session

**Saturday, 3 pm - 4 pm**

Inclusive community sessions supporting learning, social skills, and online
safety through structured play and off-screen activities.

[Elenasy]: https://elenasy.org/

## Happy New Year!

With all these events happening during the first months of the year, we hope
that everyone in Darwin Green will be off to a good start for 2026! Do you want
to make it even better? Get involved, meet people, and help organise the next
events! If you have ideas or want to give a hand, contact Sue Jackson at
<suzanne.jackson@cambridge.gov.uk>. For information about the Community Rooms
or events organised by the City Council, reach out to
<info.dgrooms@cambridge.gov.uk>. You can also use [our contact page](/contact/)
to reach out to the residents' association. Happy New Year 2026!
