## Introductions

New chair: The DGRA had invited Simon to chair the meetings as John Shuttlewood

## Action point updates

### Highway and street lighting

**Darwin Green lamp posts and lighting**: AM confirmed the contractor
instructed to undertake rolling audits and repair/replace outages. Alice
pointed out a reported outage had not been addressed since last December.
:green_circle: ACTIONS: AM to send Alice map of lamp posts; Alice to provide AM
with details of current outages and lights shining directly into bedrooms.

**Greenway temporary pedestrian/cycle route** (cut-through to Windsor Road): AM
confirmed an order had been placed to remove the white line cycle symbols and
to add pedestrian/cycle signage. Works imminent.

**Franklin Gardens: parking enforcement**: AM confirmed the parking enforcement
process - notification of residents, installation of signage and one week grace
period – will be progressed shortly.

**Franklin Garden street name signage**: Tom confirmed discussions underway
with City Council's street signage officer. :green_circle: ACTIONS Tom to
update and send Quentin the approved plan. Quentin to ask residents for any
remaining issues via WhatsApp chat.

**Franklin Gardens pedestrian and cycle links**: 1) Brownlow Road, yellow lines
to protect route from parked vehicles, 2) Blackhall Road, kerb to be removed
for smooth transition.

**BDW2 unmade up roads** (exposed iron works and steep kerbs): AM confirmed
that DWH South Midlands are programming the top surfacing works, with an
ambition these works will be completed before the New Year. Residents will be
notified of the dates with a request to remove vehicles from the highway.
Parking restrictions will be suspended to allow residents to park outside BDW2.

**BDW2 highway hazards**: :green_circle: ACTION Alice and other residents to
inform AM of any broken kerbs or other hazards.

**BDW2 Jenyns Drive** (Alice to confirm other relevant street): Highway ponding
due to blocked gutter drains. Blocked gutter drains leading to ponding.
:green_circle: ACTION To identify the precise locations, Alice to send AM
photos of the subject drains.

**BDW2 pedestrian/cycle access to Huntingdon Road**: Request for Darwin Green
sign on an existing post. AM explained further works was required on the new
route, including DDA compliance with the incline and other matters.
:green_circle: ACTION AM to report to next meeting.

**BDW2 light controlled crossing at Huntingdon Road**: Post-meeting
confirmation, there is no planning requirement for such a crossing.

**BDW2 Sedgwick Crescent parking enforcement**: AM confirmed enforcement
process to be progressed: notification of residents, installation of signage
and one week grace period.

**Lawrence Weaver Road pedestrian path**: :green_circle: ACTION John to request
landowner to remove overhanging brambles.

### Community facilities

**Franklin Gardens play area**: Tom reported seating to be installed and top
surface to be laid by end of October. :green_circle: ACTION Tom to review
location of bins and no dogs signage.

**Franklin Gardens allotments**: Tom reported Non-Material Amendment re: site
boundaries to be determined, site works to be completed by New Year, transfer
to City Council :green_circle: ACTION: AM to report on timetable for transfer
to the City Council.

### Environmental health

**Franklin Gardens (BDW6) piling**: Residents to be notified, noise and
vibration monitoring will be condition compliant.

**BDW3 spoil heap**: :green_circle: ACTIONS AM to notify residents of removal
works, dust control, monitoring and reporting arrangements and remind
contractor to water the heap.

**Sub contractor radios and language**: AM confirmed all sub-contractors are
instructed not to use radios and use bad language. :green_circle: ACTION
Resident requested to report incidents to
<Andrew.Maclaren@bdwhomes.co.uk>.

## Central Plaza review

AM announced a review to make the Plaza a more attractive and workable space.
It will address highway safety, traffic management including buses and
facilities such as parking (for medical centre, retailers), bus shelter, and
bike repair station. Sale of the six shops to be hopefully completed by the New
Year. :green_circle: ACTIONS: AM to check requirement for post box, replace bin
outside library, specify lighting for notice board.

## Any other business

Darwin Green, when live the domain name will be `www.ourdarwingreen.co.uk`

**Planning meetings**: Meetings remain on Wednesdays. Frequency becomes once
every 6 weeks.
